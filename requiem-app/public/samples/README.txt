About:
These samples are currated takes from approximately 30,000 samples for an upcoming chamber orchestra library I am working on, organized for "minimalist" contests, such as the Newgrounds Deserted Island Contest, and educational settings.

I have currated the selections to enable decent single-dynamic sustaining and multi-dynamic non-sustaining instruments, equivalent in detail to but much higher fidelity than existing soundfonts.
